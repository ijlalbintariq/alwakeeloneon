### CASE LAW ANALYSIS & RELEVANCE

The following case laws from the provided database are directly relevant to the clauses in this agreement:

1.  **[2019 SCMR 627]** — *Supreme Court of Pakistan*
    - **Facts:** A tenancy agreement was for a fixed term of 11 months. The dispute arose regarding the rights of the parties after the expiry of the fixed term.
    - **Issue:** What is the nature of the tenancy after the expiry of a fixed-term lease agreement?
    - **Held:** The Supreme Court held that after the expiry of the fixed term, the tenant does not automatically become a trespasser. The relationship of landlord and tenant continues, and the tenant can only be evicted on grounds provided by the relevant rent ordinance.
    - **Relevance:** This case is crucial for **Clause 1.2 (Renewal)** and **Clause 9 (Default and Termination)** . It reinforces that even after the initial term expires, the Lessee cannot be forcibly evicted and must be proceeded against under the Punjab Rented Premises Ordinance, 2009.

2.  **[1999 MLD 1413] & [1963 PLD 213]** — *Sindh High Court (citing PLD 1988 SC 190)*
    - **Facts:** These cases discuss the status of a tenant after the expiry of a lease agreement.
    - **Issue:** Does the tenant become a trespasser upon the expiry of the lease?
    - **Held:** The court held that upon the expiry of a lease, a new relationship comes into existence, and the tenant becomes a "statutory tenant" under the relevant Rent Ordinance. The landlord cannot take back possession by force; he must file an application for ejectment on specific grounds.
    - **Relevance:** This principle is directly incorporated into **Clause 1.2** and **Clause 9.2** of the agreement. It emphasizes that the Lessor must follow the legal process for eviction and cannot resort to self-help.

3.  **[2010 SCMR 1925]** — *Supreme Court of Pakistan*
    - **Facts:** A landlord sought eviction of a tenant on the ground of personal bonafide requirement.
    - **Issue:** What standard of proof is required from a landlord to establish a bonafide personal requirement for the premises?
    - **Held:** The Supreme Court held that the landlord's requirement is discharged the moment he appears in the witness box and makes a statement on oath or submits an affidavit to that effect. The burden then shifts to the tenant to disprove the landlord's claim.
    - **Relevance:** This case is relevant to **Clause 9.3** and the Lessor's potential future need to reclaim the shop for personal use. It provides a clear legal pathway for the Lessor to establish this ground for eviction.

4.  **[1987 PLJ 260]** — *Sindh High Court*
    - **Facts:** The case dealt with the terms of a tenancy agreement and the use of a security deposit.
    - **Issue:** Are the terms of a tenancy agreement enforceable after the expiry of the initial period? What is the purpose of a security deposit?
    - **Held:** The court held that the terms of a tenancy agreement are enforceable even after the expiry of the initial period. It also discussed that a landlord holds a security deposit to effect minor repairs necessary at the time of handing over possession.
