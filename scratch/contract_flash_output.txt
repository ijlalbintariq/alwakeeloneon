PARTNERSHIP DEED

THIS DEED OF PARTNERSHIP is made and executed on this ______ day of _______________, 20____ at Lahore, Pakistan.

BETWEEN:

1. **Mr. [Name of Partner A]**, son of [Father's Name], resident of [Address], holder of CNIC No. [__________] (hereinafter referred to as the "First Party" / "Partner A");

2. **Mr. [Name of Partner B]**, son of [Father's Name], resident of [Address], holder of CNIC No. [__________] (hereinafter referred to as the "Second Party" / "Partner B"); and

3. **Mr. [Name of Partner C]**, son of [Father's Name], resident of [Address], holder of CNIC No. [__________] (hereinafter referred to as the "Third Party" / "Partner C").

The Partners A, B, and C are collectively referred to as the "Partners" and individually as a "Partner."

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, the Partners desire to enter into a partnership for the purpose of carrying on the business of technology services, including but not limited to software development, IT consulting, digital solutions, and related activities (the "Business");

AND WHEREAS, the Partners have agreed to the terms and conditions governing their mutual rights, duties, and obligations, and to set out the same in this Deed;

NOW, THEREFORE, THIS DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

**1. COMMENCEMENT AND NAME**
1.1 The partnership shall commence on [Date] and shall be carried on under the name and style of "[Name of Firm]" (the "Firm").
1.2 The principal place of business shall be located at [Address], Lahore, or such other place as the Partners may mutually decide.

**2. NATURE OF BUSINESS**
The Firm shall engage in the business of technology services, including software development, web and mobile applications, IT infrastructure consulting, digital marketing, and any other lawful activity ancillary or incidental thereto.

**3. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS**
3.1 The initial capital of the Firm shall be Rs. [Total Capital Amount] (Rupees [amount in words] only), contributed by the Partners as follows:
   - Partner A: Rs. [_______] (representing [___]% of total capital)
   - Partner B: Rs. [_______] (representing [___]% of total capital)
   - Partner C: Rs. [_______] (representing [___]% of total capital)
3.2 The capital shall be brought in by the Partners within [30] days from the date of this Deed, in cash or by bank transfer to the Firm’s designated bank account.
3.3 Additional capital, if required, may be introduced by the Partners in their profit-sharing ratio, unless agreed otherwise in writing.
3.4 No interest shall be payable on capital unless the Partners mutually agree in writing.

**4. PROFIT AND LOSS SHARING RATIO**
4.1 The net profits and losses of the Firm shall be shared by the Partners in the following ratio:
   - Partner A: 40% (forty percent)
   - Partner B: 30% (thirty percent)
   - Partner C: 30% (thirty percent)
4.2 Profits and losses shall be determined annually after deducting all expenses, including salaries (if any), rent, operational costs, and reserves as decided by the Partners.

**5. MANAGEMENT AND AUTHORITY**
5.1 The day-to-day management and administration of the Firm shall be vested in Partner A, who shall act as the Managing Partner.
5.2 The Managing Partner shall have the authority to:
   - Open and operate bank accounts in the Firm’s name;
   - Execute contracts, agreements, and other documents in the ordinary course of business;
   - Hire and terminate employees, and determine their terms of service;
   - Incur expenses up to Rs. [Limit] per transaction without prior consent of other Partners;
   - Make decisions regarding routine operations, marketing, and client relations.
5.3 For matters exceeding the above limit, or for any decision affecting the structure, dissolution, or major financial commitment of the Firm, the unanimous written consent of all Partners shall be required.
5.4 The Managing Partner shall submit quarterly accounts and a detailed annual report to all Partners.

**6. BOOKS OF ACCOUNTS**
6.1 Proper books of account shall be maintained at the principal place of business, and every Partner shall have free access to them at all reasonable times.
6.2 The accounts shall be audited annually by a Chartered Accountant appointed by mutual agreement.

**7. BANKING**
7.1 The Firm’s bank account shall be opened with [Bank Name], Lahore, and shall be operated jointly by the Managing Partner and one other Partner (as agreed from time to time).
7.2 All cheques, bills of exchange, and other negotiable instruments shall be signed by the authorized signatories.

**8. RETIREMENT AND ADMISSION OF NEW PARTNERS**
8.1 A Partner may retire from the Firm:
   - By giving at least [90] days’ notice in writing to the other Partners;
   - With the consent of all other Partners;
   - In accordance with the provisions of the Partnership Act, 1932.
8.2 Upon retirement, the retiring Partner shall be entitled to receive the amount of his capital account, share of accumulated profits, and any other sums due, as determined by a valuation mutually agreed or by arbitration. Such amount shall be paid in [installments] as agreed.
8.3 A new Partner may be admitted only with the unanimous consent of all existing Partners and upon executing a supplementary deed.

**9. DISSOLUTION**
9.1 The Firm may be dissolved:
   - By mutual consent of all Partners;
   - Upon the occurrence of any event specified in the Partnership Act, 1932;
   - By a Partner giving [60] days’ notice of dissolution to the other Partners.
9.2 Upon dissolution, the affairs of the Firm shall be wound up, and the assets shall be distributed in accordance with the Partnership Act, 1932, and the profit-sharing ratio after settlement of all liabilities.

**10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION**
10.1 Any dispute, difference, or claim arising out of or relating to this Deed or the partnership, including its interpretation, breach, termination, or dissolution, shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Act, 1940.
10.2 The arbitration shall be conducted by a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement of the Partners. If the Partners fail to agree within 15 days, each Partner shall nominate one arbitrator, and the two arbitrators shall appoint a third arbitrator (Umpire) whose decision shall be final and binding.
10.3 The venue of arbitration shall be Lahore, Pakistan, and the language shall be Urdu or English.
10.4 The costs of arbitration shall be borne equally by the disputing parties unless otherwise decided by the arbitrator.
10.5 Subject to the arbitration clause, the courts at Lahore shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

**11. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY**
11.1 Each Partner shall indemnify the Firm and the other Partners against all losses, damages, or expenses caused by his own fraud, gross negligence, or breach of fiduciary duty.
11.2 The liability of the Partners shall be joint and several as provided under the Partnership Act, 1932.

**12. NON-COMPETE AND CONFIDENTIALITY**
12.1 During the subsistence of the partnership and for [2] years after retirement or dissolution, no Partner shall engage, directly or indirectly, in any business that is in direct competition with the Firm within the territorial limits of [Lahore Division] without the written consent of the other Partners.
12.2 All confidential information relating to the Firm’s business, clients, and operations shall be kept confidential by each Partner, both during and after the partnership.

**13. FORCE MAJEURE**
13.1 The Firm shall not be liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, riots, strikes, government restrictions, or pandemics, but the Partners shall use their best efforts to mitigate such effects.

**14. GENERAL PROVISIONS**
14.1 This Deed constitutes the entire agreement among the Partners and supersedes all prior understandings, written or oral.
14.2 No variation or amendment to this Deed shall be valid unless in writing and signed by all Partners.
14.3 If any clause is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remainder shall continue in full force and effect.
14.4 Notices under this Deed shall be in writing and delivered personally or by registered post to the addresses specified above.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**
This Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. The courts at Lahore shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matters arising hereunder.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Partners have signed this Deed on the date first above written.
