**PARTNERSHIP DEED**

THIS DEED OF PARTNERSHIP is made at Lahore on this ___ day of ________, 2025, by and amongst:

(1) MR./MS. __________________________, adult, CNIC No. ______________, resident of ______________________________________________________, hereinafter referred to as the “FIRST PARTNER” (which expression shall where the context so permits include his/her heirs, executors, administrators and assigns) of the ONE PART;

(2) MR./MS. __________________________, adult, CNIC No. ______________, resident of ______________________________________________________, hereinafter referred to as the “SECOND PARTNER” (which expression shall where the context so permits include his/her heirs, executors, administrators and assigns) of the SECOND PART;

(3) MR./MS. __________________________, adult, CNIC No. ______________, resident of ______________________________________________________, hereinafter referred to as the “THIRD PARTNER” (which expression shall where the context so permits include his/her heirs, executors, administrators and assigns) of the THIRD PART.

WHEREAS the parties hereto, being desirous of carrying on business in partnership upon the terms and conditions hereinafter contained, have mutually agreed to reduce the same into writing.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH AS UNDER:

**1. BUSINESS NAME AND NATURE**
The partnership business shall be carried on under the name and style of “___________________________” (hereinafter referred to as the “Firm”) from the principal place of business at _____________________________________, Lahore, or at such other place or places as the Partners may mutually determine from time to time. The business of the Firm shall be the provision of technology services, including but not limited to software development, IT consultancy, digital solutions, and related activities.

**2. COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION**
The partnership shall be deemed to have commenced on the ___ day of ________, 2025 and shall continue at will, subject to retirement, dissolution or reconstitution as provided herein.

**3. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS**
The capital of the Firm shall be contributed by the Partners in the following proportions and amounts, which have been brought in by respective Partners on or before the execution of this Deed:

| Partner            | Capital Contribution (Rs.) | Percentage |
|--------------------|----------------------------|------------|
| First Partner      | Rs. ____________________   | 40%        |
| Second Partner     | Rs. ____________________   | 30%        |
| Third Partner      | Rs. ____________________   | 30%        |
| **Total**          | Rs. ____________________   | 100%       |

Any further capital required for the business shall be contributed by the Partners in the same ratio or as may be mutually agreed. The capital contributions are set out in Schedule A hereto and shall be maintained in the books of account of the Firm.

**4. PROFIT AND LOSS SHARING**
The net profits and losses of the Firm shall be divided and borne by the Partners in the following shares:

- First Partner: 40%
- Second Partner: 30%
- Third Partner: 30%

Profits and losses shall be computed annually as at the close of the financial year ended on 30th June each year, after making provision for all expenses, depreciation, taxes and other liabilities, and shall be credited or debited to the respective capital accounts of the Partners.

**5. MANAGEMENT AND MANAGING PARTNER**
(a) The First Partner shall be the Managing Partner of the Firm and shall have the general management, control and supervision of the day-to-day business operations, subject to the overall supervision and direction of all Partners. The Managing Partner shall be responsible for:
   - Implementing the decisions of the Firm;
   - Supervising the accounts and maintenance of books;
   - Entering into ordinary contracts and engagements in the ordinary course of business;
   - Representing the Firm before government authorities, clients and third parties;
   - Hiring and managing employees and consultants, subject to approved budgets.

(b) The Managing Partner shall not, without the prior written consent of all other Partners:
   - Borrow money or create any charge on the assets of the Firm;
   - Enter into any contract or transaction involving an obligation exceeding Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Hundred Thousand) or such higher threshold as may be unanimously agreed from time to time;
   - Confess any judgment or refer any dispute to arbitration;
   - Sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of any substantial asset of the Firm, other than in the ordinary course of business;
   - Admit any person as a partner or create any sub-partnership.

(c) The Partners other than the Managing Partner shall be entitled to participate in all policy decisions and shall have full access to the books, records and accounts of the Firm at all reasonable times.

**6. REMUNERATION AND DRAWINGS**
No Partner shall be entitled to any salary or remuneration for acting in the business of the Firm, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Each Partner may draw, from time to time, such amount as may be unanimously approved, to be adjusted against the Partner’s share of profits. The partners may also agree on a monthly drawing limit for personal expenses, which shall be debited to the respective current accounts.

**7. BANKING AND FINANCIAL OPERATIONS**
The Firm shall maintain its accounts with ___________ Bank, Lahore, or such other bank as the Partners may decide. All cheques, drafts, payment orders and other instruments for withdrawal or operation of the bank account(s) shall be signed jointly by the Managing Partner and any one other Partner.

**8. BOOKS OF ACCOUNT AND AUDIT**
Proper books of account shall be maintained at the principal place of business, recording all receipts and payments, assets and liabilities, and all transactions of the Firm. The financial year shall end on 30th June. The accounts shall be audited annually by an independent chartered accountant appointed by the Partners. The balance sheet and profit and loss account, once signed by all Partners, shall be binding on them, save for manifest error.

**9. INSPECTION OF BOOKS**
Each Partner shall have the right to inspect and take copies of the books of account and all records of the Firm at any reasonable time.

**10. RESTRICTIONS ON PARTNERS**
No Partner shall, without the written consent of the other Partners:
   - Engage directly or indirectly in any business competing with the Firm;
   - Assign, charge or otherwise transfer his/her share in the Firm so as to make the assignee or transferee a Partner;
   - Withdraw any part of his/her capital contribution except as provided under retirement or dissolution;
   - Use the Firm’s name, goodwill, intellectual property or confidential information for personal benefit or after cessation of partnership.

**11. RETIREMENT OF A PARTNER**
(a) Any Partner may retire from the Firm by giving not less than three (3) months’ written notice to the other Partners, or such shorter period as may be mutually agreed.
(b) Upon retirement, the retiring Partner’s share in the assets, goodwill and profits shall be determined as per a valuation made as of the date of retirement. The continuing Partners shall have the option to purchase the retiring Partner’s share at the valuation price, payable within such time and in such manner as may be agreed, failing which the Firm shall be deemed dissolved.
(c) The valuation of the retiring Partner’s share shall be based on the latest audited balance sheet, adjusted for profits or losses up to the date of retirement, and shall include a reasonable valuation of goodwill, if any. In case of disagreement on goodwill valuation, the same shall be determined by an independent valuer appointed by mutual consent of all Partners.
(d) The retirement shall not relieve the retiring Partner from any antecedent liability incurred before retirement. The retiring Partner shall, if required, execute all necessary documents and give public notice of retirement.

**12. DISSOLUTION OF THE FIRM**
The Firm shall be dissolved upon:
   (a) The mutual agreement of all Partners;
   (b) The expiry of a fixed term, if any, or the completion of a specific undertaking;
   (c) The death, adjudication as insolvent or permanent incapacity of any Partner, unless the remaining Partners unanimously decide to continue the business within thirty (30) days thereof;
   (d) The happening of any event which makes it unlawful for the business to be carried on;
   (e) The order of a competent court.

Upon dissolution, the affairs of the Firm shall be wound up by the Partners or by a liquidator appointed by them. After discharging all liabilities, the net assets shall be distributed among the Partners in accordance with their respective shares in the capital and profits.

**13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION**
Any dispute, difference or controversy arising out of or in connection with this Deed, including any question regarding its existence, validity, interpretation, breach or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Act, 1940. The arbitration proceedings shall be conducted at Lahore. Each party shall appoint one arbitrator (who must be a legally qualified person of recognized standing), and the two arbitrators so appointed shall appoint a third arbitrator who shall act as the umpire. The decision of the majority of the arbitrators shall be final and binding on all Partners. The costs of the arbitration shall be borne as determined by the arbitrators. The Partners agree that no court action shall be initiated except for the enforcement of the arbitral award or for interim measures of protection.

**14. ADMISSION OF NEW PARTNER**
No person shall be admitted as a Partner in the Firm except with the unanimous consent of all the existing Partners. On admission of a new Partner, the partnership deed shall be reconstituted accordingly.

**15. EXPULSION OF A PARTNER**
A Partner may be expelled from the Firm by a unanimous vote of the other Partners if he/she:
   (a) Commits a material breach of this Deed or acts in a manner prejudicial to the interests of the Firm;
   (b) Is adjudicated bankrupt or insolvent;
   (c) Is convicted of a criminal offence involving moral turpitude;
   (d) Becomes of unsound mind.
Provided that the power of expulsion is exercised in good faith and for the benefit of the Firm, and the expelled Partner is paid his/her share as valued on the date of expulsion in the same manner as on retirement.

**16. GOODWILL AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**
The goodwill of the Firm and all intellectual property rights developed or acquired in the course of the business shall be the exclusive property of the Firm. Upon retirement or dissolution, the retiring Partner’s interest in the goodwill shall be valued and paid out as part of the settlement of accounts.

**17. MISCELLANEOUS**
(a) This Deed constitutes the entire agreement between the Partners and supersedes all prior oral or written understandings.
(b) No modification or amendment of this Deed shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by all Partners.
(c) The Partners shall, from time to time, do all such acts and execute all such further documents as may reasonably be required to give effect to the provisions of this Deed.
(d) If any provision of this Deed is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**18. GOVERNING LAW**
This Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Pakistan, including the Partnership Act, 1932 and the Contract Act, 1872.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Partners have set their respective hands on the day and year first hereinabove written.
