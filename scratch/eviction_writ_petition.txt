PETITION UNDER ARTICLE 199 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE ISLAMIC REPUBLIC OF PAKISTAN, 1973, FOR THE ISSUANCE OF A WRIT OF CERTIORARI AND/OR MANDAMUS, QUASHING THE ILLEGAL EVICTION NOTICE DATED [DATE] ISSUED BY THE RESPONDENTS AND RESTRAINING THEM FROM EVICTING THE PETITIONER FROM THE PREMISES IN QUESTION

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. That the petitioner is a lawful occupant/tenant/owner of the property bearing [complete description of property, e.g., House No. __, Street __, Sector __, Cantonment Area, City], hereinafter referred to as the "suit premises," and has been residing therein for the past [number] years.

2. That the respondents, being the Cantonment Board [Name], are a statutory body constituted under the Cantonments Act, 1924 (Act No. II of 1924), and are responsible for the administration and management of the cantonment area.

3. That on [date], the respondents issued an eviction notice bearing No. [notice number], addressed to the petitioner, directing him to vacate the suit premises within [number] days, on the alleged ground that [state the ground mentioned in the notice, e.g., the property is required for public purpose / the petitioner is an unauthorized occupant / the lease has expired, etc.].

4. That the impugned eviction notice is illegal, arbitrary, mala fide, and without lawful authority, as the same has been issued in violation of the principles of natural justice, without affording the petitioner any opportunity of being heard, and without following the procedure prescribed under the Cantonments Act, 1924 and the rules framed thereunder.

5. That the petitioner has a vested right to remain in possession of the suit premises, and the respondents have no authority to evict him except through due process of law, i.e., by obtaining a decree of eviction from a competent court of law.

6. That the impugned notice is also violative of the fundamental rights guaranteed under Articles 9 (security of person), 14 (inviolability of dignity of man), 23 (right to property), and 24 (protection of property rights) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973.

7. That the petitioner has no other adequate, efficacious, or alternative remedy available to him except to invoke the extraordinary constitutional jurisdiction of this Honourable Court under Article 199 of the Constitution of Pakistan, 1973.

8. That the cause of action arose within the territorial jurisdiction of this Honourable Court, as the suit premises are situated within the territorial limits of the Lahore High Court, and the impugned notice was issued and served upon the petitioner at [address].

9. That the petitioner has not filed any other petition or proceedings before any other court or forum in respect of the same cause of action.

10. That the valuation of this petition for the purposes of court fee and jurisdiction is fixed at Rs. [amount]. Court fee of Rs. [amount], calculated at 1% ad valorem under Article __ of Schedule II of the Court Fees Act, 1870, has been affixed herewith.

PRAYER

It is, therefore, most respectfully prayed that on acceptance of this petition, this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

(a) Issue a writ of certiorari, calling for the record of the case and quashing the impugned eviction notice dated [date] issued by the respondents, being illegal, without lawful authority, and of no legal effect.

(b) Issue a writ of mandamus, directing the respondents to refrain from evicting the petitioner from the suit premises except through due process of law.

(c) Award costs of the petition to the petitioner.

(d) Grant any other relief which this Honourable Court may deem fit and proper in the circumstances of the case.
